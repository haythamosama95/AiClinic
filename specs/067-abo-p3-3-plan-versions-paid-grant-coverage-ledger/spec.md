# Feature Specification: Plan versions, paid-grant intake and the per-clinic coverage ledger

**Feature Branch**: `ai/067-abo-p3-3-plan-versions-paid-grant-coverage-ledger`

**Created**: 2026-10-03

**Status**: Draft

**Input**: P3.3 — Plan versions, paid-grant intake and the per-clinic coverage ledger

## 1. Unit Contract

**Implements** — Read: 03 §3.1 (`term`, `grant`, `outbox` rows; `hot` fields `binding_epoch`, `clinic_seq`, `next_alarm_at`); 03 §3.2 rows `service_key`, `plan_version`, `coverage_mirror`, `coverage_event`, `grant_ledger`; 03 §3.3; 03 §5.3; 03 §5.4 (first three placement rows); 03 §6.1; 03 §6.7; 04 §1.3 rows grant (paid), getCoverage, readCoverageEvents, listGrants, register/revoke/listServiceKeys, publish/retirePlanVersion; 04 §1.4; 04 §1.6 + §1.7.

- DO SQLite tables `hot`/`term`/`grant`/`outbox`, migrated on first access (the old blob stays only for live admission until P3.4, rule S9). Worker→DO RPC carries `contract_version` (04 §7.1 row).
- `service_key` (HP register/revoke + AL-13; M `listServiceKeys`); `PLATFORM_SIGNING_KEY` signs receipts (K-3).
- `plan_version` publish/retire (HP), immutable once published.
- `grant`, paid (M): validation steps 1–3 and the binding part of step 5 (resolve/create binding); `unknown_kid` → transient, revoked → `bad_signature`; idempotency (`already_applied` with the original receipt, `conflict`); placement active/queued; receipt.
- `src/coverage/calendar.ts`: month/day arithmetic with end-of-month clamping, and `DURATION_SCALE`.
- Outbox shipped by the DO alarm to `coverage_event` (`feed_seq`), `grant_ledger`, `coverage_mirror` (higher pair only), and R2 `grant-ledger/`; AL-11 on every grant (decoded operation); AL-17 paid-grant velocity.
- `getCoverage`, `listGrants` (filters), `readCoverageEvents` (`after`, `limit ≤ 200`).

**Freezes** — `grant` (paid), `getCoverage`, `listGrants`, `readCoverageEvents`, service-key and plan-version methods; DO schema; event and receipt shapes.

**Consumes** — AI-token verification rules; `tenant_binding` model (epoch); issuer-key methods. Changing one is out of scope (rule S7).

**Open questions relied on** — None.

**Spikes** — None.

## 2. User Scenarios & Testing

### 2.1 User Story 1 - Apply a paid grant (Priority: P1)

The operator registers the ABO service key and publishes a plan version. The ABO then submits a paid grant for a clinic. The platform verifies the ABO signature, places one active term, and returns a platform-signed receipt. The same grant sent again returns that receipt. A changed allowance conflicts. A bad key or a grant outside the published plan is refused. Month arithmetic clamps the end date, and the staging duration scale shortens a month to 30 minutes. The platform worker's call into the per-clinic DO carries the current contract version, and the DO answer echoes it. A missing or unsupported version is refused before authentication and before any write.

**Why this priority**: This unit depends on P3.2, whose `tenant_binding` model is what a paid grant resolves or creates for a new org. User Story 2 reads the queue that a second grant appends, User Story 3 retires the plan under the term this story placed, and User Story 4 ships this grant's outbox. Those later stories write the DO only after this story's Worker → DO contract-version check.

**Independent Test**: E2E-P3.3-01, E2E-P3.3-02, E2E-P3.3-03, E2E-P3.3-05, E2E-P3.3-06, and E2E-P3.3-09 in harness H-AP (test clock). E2E-P3.3-11 and E2E-P3.3-12 in harness H-AP (`GatewayObject.fetch`).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** an operator publishes plan v1, **When** the ABO submits a paid grant signed with the testkit ABO key for a new org, **Then** the result is `applied`, the receipt verifies with the platform key, and the term is active with `starts_at` = now. (E2E-P3.3-01)
2. **Given** that grant was applied, **When** the same grant is sent again, **Then** the result is `already_applied` with the identical receipt, and there is one ledger row. [NFR-02, NFR-03] (E2E-P3.3-02)
3. **Given** a `grant_id` that was applied, **When** the same `grant_id` is sent with a changed allowance, **Then** the result is `conflict` and nothing changes. (E2E-P3.3-03)
4. **Given** a paid grant whose signing `kid` is not registered, **When** `grant` is called, **Then** the result is `transient` with `unknown_kid`. **Given** that `kid` is revoked, **When** `grant` is called, **Then** the result is `rejected` with `bad_signature`. (E2E-P3.3-05)
5. **Given** a paid grant for a plan version that is not published, **When** `grant` is called, **Then** the result is `plan_not_published`. **Given** `allowance_credits` above `max_allowance_per_month` × months, or `grace.days` of 8, **When** `grant` is called, **Then** the result is `exceeds_plan_bound`. **Given** a paid duration unit of `day`, **When** `grant` is called, **Then** the result is `unit_not_allowed`. **Given** `placement` `immediate`, **When** `grant` is called, **Then** the result is `placement_not_supported`. [AD-8 bound, X-06] (E2E-P3.3-06)
6. **Given** the test clock is 31 January 10:00, **When** a monthly paid grant is placed with no coverage yet, **Then** the term ends 28 February 10:00, and 29 February 10:00 in a leap year. **Given** the staging `DURATION_SCALE`, **When** a monthly term is placed, **Then** it lasts 30 minutes. (E2E-P3.3-09)
7. **Given** a Platform Worker → per-clinic DO RPC that carries the current contract version, **When** the DO handles the call, **Then** the call is accepted and the answer echoes that `contract_version`. [NFR-09] (E2E-P3.3-11)
8. **Given** a Platform Worker → per-clinic DO RPC whose `contract_version` is missing or unsupported (2 at launch), **When** the DO handles the call, **Then** the result is `rejected` with `contract_version_unsupported` and `accepted_versions`, before authentication and before any write, and nothing changes. [NFR-09] (E2E-P3.3-12)

### 2.2 User Story 2 - Read queued coverage (Priority: P2)

The ABO submits a second paid grant while the clinic already has coverage. The new term waits without dates. `getCoverage` shows one queued term and a later `coverage_through`.

**Why this priority**: The queue is visible only after User Story 1 has placed an active term. User Story 4's events describe that same coverage after the alarm.

**Independent Test**: E2E-P3.3-04 in harness H-AP.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a clinic with an active paid term, **When** a second paid grant is applied, **Then** the new term is queued without dates, and `getCoverage` shows `queued_count` 1 and `coverage_through` extended. [FR-21] (E2E-P3.3-04)

### 2.3 User Story 3 - Retire a plan version (Priority: P3)

The operator retires a published plan version. A later paid grant that names it is refused. The term already placed keeps the snapshot it stored.

**Why this priority**: Retirement is meaningful only after User Story 1 has published the version and placed a term that holds its snapshot.

**Independent Test**: E2E-P3.3-10 in harness H-AP.

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a published plan version and an active term that snapshots it, **When** `retirePlanVersion` retires that version and the next paid grant names it, **Then** that grant is refused and the existing term keeps its snapshot. (E2E-P3.3-10)

### 2.4 User Story 4 - Ship the coverage outbox (Priority: P4)

The per-clinic DO alarm ships the grant's outbox into the platform ledger, the coverage feed, the mirror, and R2, then the read API pages those events. Each applied grant sends AL-11. A fourth paid grant for one clinic inside 24 hours sends AL-17.

**Why this priority**: The alarm ships records that User Story 1's grant wrote into the outbox. The feed page is the same shipping step.

**Independent Test**: E2E-P3.3-07 and E2E-P3.3-08 in harness H-AP (test clock and DO alarm). Every earlier suite stays green (rule S2).

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a paid grant has been applied, **When** the DO alarm runs, **Then** `coverage_event` rows exist with rising `clinic_seq`, a `grant_ledger` row exists, `coverage_mirror` is updated, an R2 object exists, and `readCoverageEvents` pages in `feed_seq` order. (E2E-P3.3-07)
2. **Given** a paid grant is applied, **When** the alarm ships the outbox, **Then** the captured email is AL-11 and carries the decoded operation and the org. **Given** a fourth paid grant for one clinic within 24 hours, **When** that grant is applied, **Then** the platform raises AL-17. (E2E-P3.3-08)

### 2.6 Edge Cases

- An unknown signing `kid` answers `transient` with `detail` `unknown_kid`. A `transient` result changes nothing. A `kid` with `status` `revoked`, or an expired `kid`, answers `rejected` with `bad_signature`. A `kid` is expired when `status` is `active` and now is before `not_before` or after `not_after`. Expired is not a stored status. (E2E-P3.3-05, 04 §1.4, 03 §3.2)
- The grant envelope's canonical hash must match the signed hash. A mismatch answers `rejected` with `bad_signature`. Every grant (`paid`, `complimentary`, and `transfer`) requires `evidence.approvals` to hold at least 1 element. A shorter list answers `rejected` with `approvals_required` and `detail` empty. (04 §1.4)
- A plan version that is not `published`, including a `retired` version, fails validation as `plan_not_published`. `allowance_credits` above `max_allowance_per_month` × months, or paid grace that breaks the grace rule, answers `exceeds_plan_bound`. A paid unit other than `month`, including `day`, answers `unit_not_allowed`. `placement` `immediate` or `replace` answers `placement_not_supported`. These bounds are what a compromised ABO can fabricate. [AD-8 bound, X-06] (E2E-P3.3-06, E2E-P3.3-10, 04 §1.4)
- Paid grace is `days` ≤ `max_paid_grace_days` (7) and `cap_rule` `proportional`. Eight grace days answers `exceeds_plan_bound`. (04 §1.4, E2E-P3.3-06)
- Paid duration is `month` with count 1, 3, or 12. `allowance_credits` is an integer ≥ 1. (04 §1.4)
- The same `grant_id` with the same envelope hash answers `already_applied` and returns the original receipt. The same `grant_id` with a different envelope hash answers `conflict` and changes nothing. (E2E-P3.3-02, E2E-P3.3-03, 04 §1.3)
- There is no `not_pending` refusal. A term grant becomes active when there is no active, grace, or unheld queued term. It becomes active with a backdated calendar during grace. Otherwise it queues at the end. (03 §6.1, 03 §5.4)
- A grant with no active, grace, or unheld queued term becomes `active` with `starts_at` = `calendar_start` = now. A grant while an active or queued term exists becomes `queued` and is appended at the end of the queue. A grant during grace becomes `active` with `calendar_start` equal to the grace term's `ends_at`, and the grace term ends `renewed`. (03 §5.4, E2E-P3.3-01, E2E-P3.3-04)
- Queued terms store a duration, not dates. They get dates when they activate. (03 §6.1, E2E-P3.3-04)
- `ends_at` = add(`calendar_start`, `duration_unit`, `duration_count`). Months keep the same day-of-month and time in UTC, clamped to the last day of a shorter month. A grant at 31 January 10:00 ends 28 February 10:00, and 29 February 10:00 in a leap year. Days are exact multiples of 24 hours. (03 §6.1, E2E-P3.3-09)
- Staging `DURATION_SCALE` maps 1 month to 30 minutes and 1 day to 1 minute. Every duration is computed in unscaled units and then scaled. Production has no scale. (03 §6.1, E2E-P3.3-09)
- A paid `term_adjustment` source is rejected at launch. `placement` `immediate` is rejected at launch. (03 §5.3, 04 §1.4)
- More than 3 paid grants for one clinic in 24 hours raises AL-17. The 4th paid grant for one clinic within 24 hours is that condition. More than 20 paid grants across all clinics in an hour raises the same alert. The grant is still applied. (04 §1.4, E2E-P3.3-08, 05 §2)
- `coverage_mirror` is replaced only by a higher `(binding_epoch, clinic_seq)` pair. The alarm uses `INSERT OR IGNORE` on event id or `request_id`, then deletes the shipped outbox rows. `setAlarm` is called only when the alarm time moves. `coverage_event.kind` is `grant_applied`, `grant_voided`, `term_activated`, `term_ended`, `term_held`, `term_released`, `grace_started`, `band_crossed`, `suspension_changed`, or `transfer`. A paid grant emits `grant_applied`, and also `term_activated` when placement leaves the new term `active`, and `term_ended` when placement ends the grace term. A grant that only queues emits `grant_applied` alone. Those events are written in the order `term_ended`, `term_activated`, `grant_applied`, skipping any the grant does not emit. (03 §6.7, E2E-P3.3-07)
- `readCoverageEvents` takes `after` and `limit`. A successful call is `ok` with `code` empty and `receipt` absent. `detail` is the JSON text of `{after, events, next_after, has_more}`. Events are unsigned, have `feed_seq` greater than `after`, and are in ascending `feed_seq`, at most `limit`. `next_after` is the last returned `feed_seq`, or `after` when the page is empty. `has_more` is true when a further event exists. A missing `limit`, or a `limit` outside 1 through 200, is `rejected` with `limit_invalid` and `detail` empty, and no events are returned. A `limit` of 200 is accepted. (04 §1.3, 04 §4.1, E2E-P3.3-07)
- A Platform Worker → per-clinic DO RPC that carries the current `contract_version` is accepted, and the DO answer echoes that same `contract_version`. The DO accepts N and N−1 and answers in the version the call used. (04 §7.1, 04 §7.2, E2E-P3.3-11)
- A Platform Worker → per-clinic DO RPC whose `contract_version` is missing or unsupported (2 at launch, because every channel is version 1) is `rejected` with `contract_version_unsupported` and `accepted_versions`. The check runs before authentication and before any write, so nothing changes. (04 §7.1, 04 §7.2, E2E-P3.3-12)

## 3. Requirements

### 3.1 Functional Requirements

- **FR-001**: On first access the per-clinic DO migrates to SQLite tables `hot`, `term`, `grant`, and `outbox`. The existing JSON blob stays only for live admission until P3.4. (03 §3.1, 06 §2 S9)
- **FR-002**: `hot` includes `binding_epoch`, `clinic_seq`, and `next_alarm_at`. `term` is one row per term and stores `term_id`, `grant_id`, `origin_grant_id`, `position`, `state`, `end_reason`, `plan_snapshot` (JSON object `{plan_id, version, display_name, capabilities, max_cost_class, concurrency_limit}`; `capabilities` is that version's JSON array of capability id strings, copied unchanged), `allowance`, `used_final`, `duration_unit`, `duration_count`, `grace_days`, `grace_cap`, `calendar_start`, `starts_at`, `ends_at`, `grace_ends_at`, and `ended_at`. `grant` is one row per grant and stores `grant_id`, `kind` (`term`, `term_adjustment`), `source_kind`, `envelope_sha256`, `envelope`, `evidence`, `receipt`, `applied_at`, `voided_at`, and `void_reason`. `outbox` is transient and stores `seq`, `kind` (`coverage_event`, `grant_ledger`, `usage_adjustment`, `alert`), and `payload`. The alarm ships outbox rows to D1 and then deletes them. (03 §3.1)
- **FR-003**: The Platform Worker → per-clinic DO RPC carries `contract_version` on every call and every answer. The DO accepts the current version and N−1 and answers in the version the call used, so a call that carries the current version is accepted and the answer echoes that `contract_version`. A call whose `contract_version` is missing or outside N and N−1 (2 at launch, because every channel is version 1) is `rejected` with `contract_version_unsupported` and `accepted_versions`. That check runs before authentication and before any write, so nothing changes. (04 §7.1, 04 §7.2, E2E-P3.3-11, E2E-P3.3-12)
- **FR-004**: `service_key` stores `kid`, `service` (`abo`), `public_key`, `status` (`active`, `revoked`), `not_before`, `not_after`, `registered_by`, and `assertion_sha256`. `public_key` is the base64url encoding of the raw 32-byte Ed25519 public key. `not_before` and `not_after` are the validity, UTC ISO-8601 timestamps. A `kid` is expired when `status` is `active` and now is before `not_before` or after `not_after`. Expired is not a stored status, and there is no `retiring` status. `registerServiceKey` and `revokeServiceKey` are class HP. They do not record a grant or a reversal. A successful call is `ok`: `code` is empty, `receipt` is absent, and `detail` is the JSON text of the `service_key` row. `registerServiceKey` takes `kid`, `public_key`, `not_before`, and `not_after`. It inserts `status` `active`, sets `service` to `abo`, stores `public_key` unchanged, sets `registered_by` to the Access email, and sets `assertion_sha256` to the assertion challenge hash. The same `kid` with the same `public_key` is `ok` again and does not insert another row or change `status` or the stored validity. The same `kid` with a different `public_key` is `conflict` with code `public_key_mismatch` and `detail` empty. A `public_key` that is not that encoding is `rejected` with code `public_key_invalid` and `detail` empty. `revokeServiceKey` takes `kid`. It sets `status` to `revoked`, and it is `ok` when the row is already `revoked`. It is the only writer of `revoked`. A missing `kid` is `rejected` with code `kid_not_found` and `detail` empty. Each registration and revocation raises AL-13. AL-13's body carries the decoded operation and its target, and the repeat is once. `listServiceKeys` is class M. A successful call is `ok`: `code` is empty, `receipt` is absent, and `detail` is the JSON text of an array of `{kid, status, not_before, not_after}` for every `service_key` row, including `revoked`. (03 §3.2, 04 §1.2, 04 §1.3, 05 §2, 02 §6 K-4)
- **FR-005**: `PLATFORM_SIGNING_KEY` signs receipts (K-3). The receipt is `{contract_version, grant_id or reversal_id, installation_id, org_id, result, term_ids, applied_at, ledger_seq, envelope_sha256, kid, signature}`. The signature is by the platform key over the canonical receipt without the signature. `signature` is a compact JWS (RFC 7515): header `{alg: "EdDSA", kid}` naming that platform key, payload the RFC 8785 canonical receipt with the `signature` member omitted, and the third segment the base64url Ed25519 signature. `ledger_seq` is the integer `clinic_seq` on the coverage event for this grant (`kind` `grant_applied`) or void (`kind` `grant_voided`). It is not a `grant_ledger` column. Both sides store the receipt. `receipt` is present when `result` is `applied` or `already_applied`. (04 §1.6, 02 §6 K-3, E2E-P3.3-01)
- **FR-006**: `plan_version` stores `plan_id`, `version`, `display_name`, `capabilities`, `max_cost_class`, `concurrency_limit`, `max_allowance_per_month`, `status` (`published`, `retired`), `published_by`, and `assertion_sha256`. `capabilities` is a JSON array of capability id strings. Content fields are immutable once published. `publishPlanVersion` and `retirePlanVersion` are class HP. They do not record a grant or a reversal. A successful call is `ok`: `code` is empty, `receipt` is absent, and `detail` is the JSON text of the `plan_version` row. `publishPlanVersion` takes `plan_id`, `version`, `display_name`, `capabilities`, `max_cost_class`, `concurrency_limit`, and `max_allowance_per_month`. It inserts `status` `published`, sets `published_by` to the Access email, and sets `assertion_sha256` to the assertion challenge hash. The same `(plan_id, version)` with the same values in those content fields is `ok` again and does not insert another row or change `status` or those fields. The same pair with a different value in any of those fields is `conflict` with code `plan_version_mismatch` and `detail` empty, and the stored row is unchanged. `retirePlanVersion` takes `plan_id` and `version`. It sets `status` from `published` to `retired`, and it is `ok` when the row is already `retired`. It is the only writer of `retired`. A missing `(plan_id, version)` is `rejected` with code `plan_version_not_found` and `detail` empty. (03 §3.2, 04 §1.2, 04 §1.3)
- **FR-007**: Paid `grant` is class M. Beyond `contract_version` the input is the grant envelope, `abo_kid`, and `abo_signature`. `source.kind` `paid` selects class M and an ABO signature, and the effect is to append a term. The platform validates in order and answers `rejected` with the named code: (1) `contract_version` is supported and the envelope's canonical hash matches the signed hash (`bad_signature`); every grant (`paid`, `complimentary`, and `transfer`) requires `evidence.approvals` to hold at least 1 element, and a shorter list is `rejected` with `approvals_required` and `detail` empty; (2) the ABO signature verifies against the named `service_key` — an unknown `kid` answers `transient` with `detail` `unknown_kid`, and a revoked or expired `kid` answers `rejected` with `bad_signature`; (3) the plan version is `published` (`plan_not_published`), the source, unit, and count are allowed (`unit_not_allowed`), and for `paid`, `allowance_credits` ≤ `max_allowance_per_month` × months and grace follows the grace row (`exceeds_plan_bound`). `placement` `immediate` and `replace` are rejected with `placement_not_supported`. Paid duration is `{unit: month, count: 1, 3, or 12}`. Paid grace is `{days ≤ 7, cap_rule: proportional}`. The binding part of step 5 resolves or creates the active binding for `org_id`. (03 §5.3, 04 §1.3, 04 §1.4, E2E-P3.3-05, E2E-P3.3-06)
- **FR-008**: `grant` is idempotent by `grant_id` plus `envelope_sha256`. The same id with the same content hash returns `already_applied` and the original receipt, and leaves one ledger row. The same id with a different content hash returns `conflict` and changes nothing. On the platform a grant applied by this unit is `applied`. (04 §1.3, 03 §5.3, E2E-P3.3-02, E2E-P3.3-03)
- **FR-009**: The paid grant envelope carries `contract_version`, `grant_id` (hex SHA-256), `org_id`, `kind` (`term` or `term_adjustment`), `placement` (`queue` at launch), `source` (`{kind, ref, operator_email, reason}`; `operator_email` and `reason` are required unless `kind` is `paid`; `ref` is the ABO's `payment_id` and never a provider id), `plan` (`{plan_id, plan_version}`), `duration`, `allowance_credits` (integer ≥ 1), `grace`, `paid_at`, and `evidence` (`{content_sha256, approvals[]}`; for `paid`, `content_sha256` is the hash of the ABO payment fact and its evidence; `approvals` holds operator assertions; the minimum of 1 binds every grant, `paid`, `complimentary`, and `transfer`; a shorter list is `rejected` with `approvals_required` and `detail` empty). The platform snapshots the published plan version itself. (04 §1.4)
- **FR-010**: A term grant becomes active if there is no active, grace, or unheld queued term, with `starts_at` = `calendar_start` = now. It becomes queued, appended at the end, while an active or queued term exists, and a queued term stores a duration, not dates. A grant during grace becomes active with `calendar_start` equal to the grace term's `ends_at`, and that grace term ends `renewed`. `ends_at` = add(`calendar_start`, `duration_unit`, `duration_count`), with end-of-month clamping in UTC. Staging `DURATION_SCALE` maps 1 month to 30 minutes and 1 day to 1 minute; durations are computed unscaled, then scaled. Production has no scale. `src/coverage/calendar.ts` is that month/day arithmetic. There is no `not_pending` refusal. (03 §5.4, 03 §6.1, E2E-P3.3-01, E2E-P3.3-04, E2E-P3.3-09)
- **FR-011**: On success the DO applies the grant atomically, stores the envelope and evidence, and emits the ledger mirror, the coverage event, and the out-of-band alert through its outbox. The alarm is set to the earliest of the next boundary (`ends_at`, `grace_ends_at`) or a pending outbox, and `setAlarm` runs only when that time moves. The alarm ships the outbox to D1 (`coverage_event`, `grant_ledger`, `grant_void`, `coverage_mirror`, `usage_event` for `usage_adjustment`, `platform_alert`) with `INSERT OR IGNORE` on event id or `request_id`, then deletes the shipped rows, and writes the R2 `grant-ledger/` object. `coverage_mirror` is replaced only by a higher `(binding_epoch, clinic_seq)`. Events are emitted through the outbox, and each event carries the clinic's full coverage snapshot after the change. `coverage_event.kind` is `grant_applied`, `grant_voided`, `term_activated`, `term_ended`, `term_held`, `term_released`, `grace_started`, `band_crossed`, `suspension_changed`, or `transfer`. `band_crossed` is both the 75 % crossing and the 90 % crossing; the snapshot carries the band. A paid grant emits `grant_applied`, and also `term_activated` when placement leaves the new term `active`, and `term_ended` when placement ends the grace term. A grant that only queues emits `grant_applied` alone. Those events are written in the order `term_ended`, `term_activated`, `grant_applied`, skipping any the grant does not emit. For a paid grant the snapshot is the post-grant snapshot, its `clinic_seq` equals that event's `clinic_seq`, and `at` is the UTC ISO-8601 time of the grant. `coverage_event` stores `feed_seq` (autoincrement, the global cursor), `event_id` (unique), `org_id`, `installation_id`, `binding_epoch`, `clinic_seq`, `kind`, `snapshot`, and `at`, and is never purged. (04 §1.4, 03 §3.2, 03 §3.3, 03 §6.7, 04 §4.1, E2E-P3.3-07)
- **FR-012**: `grant_ledger` stores `grant_id`, `origin_grant_id`, `org_id`, `installation_id`, `kind`, `source_kind`, `operator_credential_id`, `envelope_sha256`, `receipt`, and `applied_at`. It is append-only, with indexes on `(org_id, applied_at)`, `(origin_grant_id)`, and `(operator_credential_id, applied_at)`. `coverage_mirror` stores `installation_id`, `org_id`, `binding_epoch`, `clinic_seq`, `state`, `suspended`, `hard_stop_at`, and `term_snapshot`, and is written only on events. R2 `grant-ledger/` holds one NDJSON object per grant and per void, under a bucket lock. A grant is the object `grant-ledger/<grant_id>.ndjson`: one JSON line `{grant_id, origin_grant_id, org_id, installation_id, kind, source_kind, operator_credential_id, envelope_sha256, applied_at, receipt}`, where `receipt` is the §1.6 receipt and the other fields are the `grant_ledger` columns. That object is what a rebuild copies into `grant_ledger`. A void is a different object, `grant-ledger/<grant_id>.void.ndjson`: one JSON line `{grant_id, reason, source, evidence_sha256, at, receipt}`. It does not replace the grant object. (03 §3.2, 03 §3.3, E2E-P3.3-07)
- **FR-013**: Every grant alerts, paid grants included (AL-11). AL-11 is raised once. The body carries the decoded operation and its target; for a paid grant that target includes the org. Paid grants also feed a velocity check: more than 3 paid grants for one clinic in 24 hours, or more than 20 across all clinics in an hour, raises AL-17. AL-17 repeats hourly. The 4th paid grant for one clinic within 24 hours raises AL-17. (04 §1.4, 05 §2, E2E-P3.3-08)
- **FR-014**: `getCoverage` is class M. Beyond `contract_version` it takes `org_id`. A successful call is `ok`: `code` is empty, `receipt` is absent, and `detail` is the JSON text of `{snapshot, queued_terms, recent_terms}`. The snapshot carries `contract_version`, `state`, `reason` (`none`, `expired`, `grace_exhausted`, `exhausted`, `reversed`, `transferred`, `transfer_pending`) for a non-available state, `suspended`, `term` (null, or `{ref, plan_display_name, starts_at, ends_at, grace_ends_at, allowance, used, band}` with `band` `ok`, `75`, `90`, or `exhausted`), `queued_count` (unheld queued terms), `held_count`, `coverage_through`, `binding_epoch`, and `clinic_seq`. `coverage_through` is the projected end of coverage assuming no exhaustion. The snapshot has no prices, payment references, or provider ids. `queued_terms` are the unheld terms in state `queued`, in ascending `position`. Each is `{plan_id, plan_version, plan_display_name, duration_unit, duration_count}` and has no dates. `recent_terms` are at most the last 12 terms whose state is `active`, `grace`, or `ended`, highest `position` first. Fewer than 12 returns those that exist. Each is `{term_id, state, plan_display_name, starts_at, ends_at, grace_ends_at, allowance, used}`. `used` is `hot.used` when the term is `hot.active_term_id`, and `used_final` otherwise. A second paid grant leaves `queued_count` 1 and extends `coverage_through`. (04 §1.2, 04 §1.3, 04 §1.7, E2E-P3.3-04)
- **FR-015**: `retirePlanVersion` retires a published `plan_version`. The next paid grant that names that version is refused. The existing term keeps its `plan_snapshot`. (03 §3.2, 04 §1.4, E2E-P3.3-10)
- **FR-016**: `listGrants` is class M. Beyond `contract_version` the filters are `org_id`, `source_kind`, `credential_id`, `applied_from`, and `applied_to`. `credential_id` matches `operator_credential_id`. `applied_from` and `applied_to` are inclusive UTC ISO-8601 bounds on `applied_at`. An omitted filter does not constrain that column. A successful call is `ok`: `code` is empty, the envelope `receipt` is absent, and `detail` is the JSON text of the matching `grant_ledger` rows, ordered by `applied_at` then `grant_id` ascending. Each row's `receipt` is the §1.6 receipt object. `readCoverageEvents` is class M. Beyond `contract_version` it takes `after` and `limit`. A successful call is `ok`: `code` is empty, `receipt` is absent, and `detail` is the JSON text of `{after, events, next_after, has_more}`. Each event is `{event_id, feed_seq, org_id, installation_id, binding_epoch, clinic_seq, kind, at, snapshot}`, unsigned. The page contains events with `feed_seq` greater than `after`, in ascending `feed_seq`, at most `limit`. `after` echoes the caller's cursor. `next_after` is the last returned `feed_seq`, or `after` when the page is empty. `has_more` is true when a further event exists. A missing `limit`, or a `limit` outside 1 through 200, is `rejected` with code `limit_invalid` and `detail` empty, and no events are returned. A `limit` of 200 is accepted. (04 §1.2, 04 §1.3, 04 §4.1, 03 §3.2, E2E-P3.3-07)

### 3.2 Key Entities

- **`service_key`**: D1 row for an ABO signing key. Fields: `kid`, `service` (`abo`), `public_key` (base64url of the raw 32-byte Ed25519 public key), `status` (`active`, `revoked`), `not_before`, `not_after` (validity, UTC ISO-8601), `registered_by`, `assertion_sha256`. `registerServiceKey` inserts `status` `active`. `revokeServiceKey` is the only writer of `revoked`. There is no `retiring` status. (03 §3.2, 04 §1.3)
- **`plan_version`**: D1 row for one published plan version. Fields: `plan_id`, `version`, `display_name`, `capabilities` (a JSON array of capability id strings), `max_cost_class`, `concurrency_limit`, `max_allowance_per_month`, `status` (`published`, `retired`), `published_by`, `assertion_sha256`. Content fields are immutable once published. `publishPlanVersion` inserts `status` `published`. `retirePlanVersion` is the only writer of `retired`. (03 §3.2)
- **`hot` / `term` / `grant` / `outbox`**: Per-clinic DO SQLite tables. This unit's `hot` fields are `binding_epoch`, `clinic_seq`, and `next_alarm_at`. `term`, `grant`, and `outbox` use the fields in FR-002. (03 §3.1)
- **`coverage_event`**: Append-only D1 feed row keyed by `feed_seq`, never purged. `kind` is `grant_applied`, `grant_voided`, `term_activated`, `term_ended`, `term_held`, `term_released`, `grace_started`, `band_crossed`, `suspension_changed`, or `transfer`. A paid grant writes `grant_applied`, and `term_activated` or `term_ended` when placement says so, in the order `term_ended`, `term_activated`, `grant_applied`, skipping any it does not emit. (03 §3.2, 03 §6.7)
- **`grant_ledger`**: Append-only D1 ledger row for an applied grant, including the receipt. (03 §3.2)
- **`coverage_mirror`**: D1 row written only on events, replaced only by a higher `(binding_epoch, clinic_seq)`. (03 §3.2, 03 §6.7)
- **R2 `grant-ledger/` object**: One NDJSON object per grant and per void, under a bucket lock. A grant is `grant-ledger/<grant_id>.ndjson`: one JSON line `{grant_id, origin_grant_id, org_id, installation_id, kind, source_kind, operator_credential_id, envelope_sha256, applied_at, receipt}`, where `receipt` is the §1.6 receipt and the other fields are the `grant_ledger` columns. A rebuild copies that line into `grant_ledger`. A void is `grant-ledger/<grant_id>.void.ndjson`: one JSON line `{grant_id, reason, source, evidence_sha256, at, receipt}`, and it does not replace the grant object. (03 §3.3)
- **Grant envelope**: The paid-grant argument object in FR-009. Idempotent by `grant_id` plus `envelope_sha256`. (04 §1.4, 04 §1.3)
- **Receipt**: The platform-signed object in FR-005. (04 §1.6)
- **Coverage snapshot**: The object in FR-014, carried on `getCoverage` and on each coverage event. (04 §1.7, 03 §6.7)

## 4. Constitution Alignment

### 4.1 Architecture & Operations Impact

- **Clinic Fit**: One coverage ledger per clinic org, held on the AI platform the clinic already calls. The unit sizes terms, queues, and plan bounds for that org. It adds no second clinic product and no hospital-scale control plane.
- **Layer Placement**: Codebase is `ai-platform` only. No wiring exception is named. Live entries are `VendorEntrypoint` methods `registerServiceKey`, `revokeServiceKey`, `listServiceKeys`, `publishPlanVersion`, `retirePlanVersion`, `grant`, `getCoverage`, `listGrants`, and `readCoverageEvents` over the H-AP self service binding (`vendorCall` in `ai-platform/test/system/harness.ts`), `GatewayObject.fetch` (the Platform Worker → per-clinic DO RPC) on the real DO binding, and the `GatewayObject` DO alarm fired with `runDurableObjectAlarm` (rule V4). `plan.md` re-runs the constitution check in 02 §7 (rule S12).
- **Data Integrity & Security**: Authority for these records is the per-clinic DO (`hot`, `term`, `grant`, `outbox`), platform D1 (`service_key`, `plan_version`, `coverage_mirror`, `coverage_event`, `grant_ledger`), and R2 `grant-ledger/`. A paid grant is class M and is accepted only with a valid ABO signature and inside the published plan bounds. Plan publish, plan retire, service-key register, and service-key revoke are class HP and raise AL-13 on register and revoke. Receipts are signed by `PLATFORM_SIGNING_KEY`. The grant resolves or creates the active binding and does not change the consumed `tenant_binding` model. The JSON blob stays for live admission until P3.4. (03 §3.1, 03 §3.2, 03 §3.3, 04 §1.4, 04 §1.6)
- **Failure Handling**: Unknown `kid` returns `transient` `unknown_kid` and writes nothing (E2E-P3.3-05, 04 §1.4). A `kid` with `status` `revoked`, or an expired `kid`, returns `rejected` `bad_signature` (E2E-P3.3-05, 04 §1.4). A `kid` is expired when `status` is `active` and now is before `not_before` or after `not_after`. Expired is not a stored status. A shorter `evidence.approvals` list returns `rejected` `approvals_required` with `detail` empty and writes nothing (04 §1.4). Unpublished plan, allowance or grace over the plan bound, paid unit `day`, and `placement` `immediate` return `plan_not_published`, `exceeds_plan_bound`, `unit_not_allowed`, and `placement_not_supported` (E2E-P3.3-06). A changed allowance for an existing `grant_id` returns `conflict` and changes nothing (E2E-P3.3-03). A Worker → DO RPC whose `contract_version` is missing or unsupported returns `rejected` `contract_version_unsupported` with `accepted_versions` before authentication and before any write (E2E-P3.3-12, 04 §7.1). The alarm ships with `INSERT OR IGNORE` and replaces `coverage_mirror` only for a higher `(binding_epoch, clinic_seq)` (03 §6.7, E2E-P3.3-07). A paid grant emits `coverage_event.kind` `grant_applied`, plus `term_activated` when the new term is `active`, plus `term_ended` when placement ends the grace term, written in the order `term_ended`, `term_activated`, `grant_applied`, skipping any the grant does not emit. A grant that only queues emits `grant_applied` alone (03 §6.7).

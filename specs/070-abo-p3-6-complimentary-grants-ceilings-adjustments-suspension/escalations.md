# P3.6 escalations

Assumptions chosen by the resolver. Each one is written into the design docs named below.

## 1. ceiling_policy columns, setCeilingPolicy, and the 90-day window

**Question:** `ceiling_policy` is versioned and states the numeric ceilings plus `set_by` and `assertion_sha256`, and `setCeilingPolicy` takes “Ceiling fields” and returns a policy version, but the cited sections do not name the stored columns, the method inputs, or the returned version, and they do not say how the per-clinic 90-day window is anchored or how a `month` complimentary grant counts toward the 62-day cap.

**Assumption:** `ceiling_policy` stores `version`, `per_grant_max_days`, `per_grant_max_allowance_months`, `window_days`, `window_max_days`, `window_max_allowance_months`, `max_paid_grace_days`, `paid_cap_rule`, `set_by`, and `assertion_sha256`. `version` is an integer: 1 for the first row, then one greater for each later row. The greatest `version` is the policy in force. Launch values are 31, 1, 90, 62, 2, 7, and `proportional`, which are the ceilings already stated for a complimentary grant, the per-clinic window, and paid grace. `setCeilingPolicy` takes those ceiling fields (HP already supplies `access_jwt` and `assertion`). On `ok` it inserts the next `version`, sets `set_by` to the Access email and `assertion_sha256` to the assertion challenge hash, and stores the supplied fields. The same assertion challenge is `ok` again and does not insert another row. `detail` is the JSON text of that row; its `version` is the returned policy version. `code` is empty and `receipt` is absent. The per-clinic window is anchored at this grant's `applied_at` (the time of the check) for its `org_id`: `window_days` × 24 hours ending at that instant. Earlier complimentary grants and `term_adjustment` rows for that `org_id` count when their `applied_at` is greater than this `applied_at` minus that span and less than or equal to this `applied_at`. A complimentary `duration` of `day` contributes `count` days toward the per-grant and window day ceilings. A `month` contributes `count` × 31 days toward those same day ceilings. Allowance stays in months of the plan's `max_allowance_per_month`.

**Why:** 01 I-10 and 03 §3.2 already fix the launch numbers (≤ 31 days and ≤ 1 month of allowance per grant; ≤ 62 days and ≤ 2 months of allowance per clinic per 90 days; paid `grace.days` ≤ 7 and `cap_rule = proportional`) and already name `set_by` and `assertion_sha256`. 04 §1.3 already returns a policy version and is idempotent by assertion challenge, and sibling HP writes store the Access email and the assertion challenge hash and return the row as `detail`. `grant_ledger` is already indexed by `(org_id, applied_at)`, and 03 §6.1 defines a day as 24 hours. A queued complimentary term has no `calendar_start` at validation, so a `month` cannot be measured from the calendar. Counting each month as 31 days uses the per-grant day ceiling already stated, so a calendar month cannot sit under the 62-day cap.

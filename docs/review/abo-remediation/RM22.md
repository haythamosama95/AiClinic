# RM22 — Term-local accounting

## Findings implemented

- **R5-A1 (P1):** Admission allowance, grace usage, allowance bands, exhaustion, and coverage snapshots now count reservations by their original `term_id`. The global reservation count remains the concurrency limit. Existing reservations retain their original term attribution across activation.
- **R5-A2 (P1):** Exhaustion and grace exhaustion close terms with settled `hot.used`; live reservations remain in the hot row. Consumed and stale reservations update their original active or ended term exactly once, while release and zero-capacity settlement do not change settled usage. Grant void and grant-void rebuild paths also freeze settled usage. A forward JSON migration subtracts still-live, term-attributed reservations from retained exhausted rows once; ambiguous historical attribution is left for request-fact reconciliation.
- **R5-A7 (P2):** New never-admitted refusals are not stored in replay or idempotency maps and do not write hot state when no boundary or stale-reservation change occurred. Loading hot state removes historical refusal answers while preserving admitted and terminal bindings.
- No recipe steps were intentionally deferred. No migration or schema change was added.

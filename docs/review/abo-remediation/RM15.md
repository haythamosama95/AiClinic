# RM15 — Atomic DO grant commit and durable wake-up

## Findings implemented

- **R4-D1 (P1):** Paid, complimentary, and term-adjustment grants prepare the term mutation, final hot/term state, signed receipt, and ordered coverage events before committing them together in the Durable Object storage transaction. Event snapshots reflect the final state and the grant receipt uses the `grant_applied` sequence. The paid velocity observation is now durable outbox work, so its read cannot strand a committed grant before scheduling a wake-up.
- **R4-D4 (P1):** New grants prearm a near-term Durable Object alarm before the atomic write. `syncAlarm` compares the desired wake-up with `storage.getAlarm()` and repairs missing or late alarms; the hot-row alarm timestamp is treated as a desired-state cache. Exact grant replay checks term reconciliation and repairs the durable alarm before returning the original receipt.
- No recipe steps were deferred. No new migration or precedence conflict was needed.

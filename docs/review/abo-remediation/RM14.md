# RM14 — Global grant claims and route seals

## Findings implemented

- **R4-PG1 (P1):** Platform D1 now owns a global claim for each grant ID, including paid, complimentary, and transfer-generated IDs. Claims bind organization, source, canonical envelope/hash, and the installation/epoch/route generation. Authenticated exact completed replay returns the original receipt; changed organization or envelope conflicts. Pending claims dispatch only on their stored route. A pending claim can move only after the old DO atomically returns a no-effect route seal; the claim update is compare-and-swap fenced by the original route and generation. The migration backfill compares retained DO/ledger identities and fails closed, disputing and alerting on ambiguity rather than selecting or recreating value (D-5).
- **R4-PG2 (P1):** Paid and complimentary DO calls use the RM07 response decoder. Transport or unknown outcomes remain transient; explicit version refusals and actual business validation refusals retain their specified classification. Receipt identity is checked against the exact claim route before completion.
- **04 §1.4:** Documented the authenticated exact-replay exception after envelope validation/hash. It does not authorize a new application against a retired plan.

## Seams, discovered issues and precedence

- **Transfer-step crash window:** `transferIn` can apply in the destination DO before the D1 `transfer_step` row is committed. That aggregate saga window is beyond R4-PG1 and remains for the transfer lifecycle owner; RM14 does not broaden its implementation to solve it.
- **D-5 operations:** If retained historical identities or receipts disagree, the migration quarantines the claim and blocks dependent mutations. Reconciliation and any value-affecting resolution require retained evidence and the append-only HP adjudication path; no winner is inferred.
- No phase findings are deferred. No precedence conflict remains. Existing RM07 decoder classifications are reused.

## Operator checklist

3. Deploy the receiver schema/seal support with the dispatcher. No new secret, binding, service, or queue is introduced.

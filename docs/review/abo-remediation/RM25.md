# RM25 — Fallback settlement and usage journal

## Findings implemented

- **R5-A3:** Added durable, idempotent fallback settlement receipts shared with normal admission, including replay-safe draining, concurrency handling, and retry after partial failure.
- **R5-A6:** Reconciliation now closes fallback rows from an exact durable usage fact and does not synthesize a charge when the accounting record is absent.
- **R5-A5:** Exhausting an active term records the terminal accounting result and activates its queued successor once, including across receipt replay.
- **R5-J1:** Terminal accounting intent is persisted before usage insertion and acknowledged only after durable usage is present. Release, stale adjustment, and outage replay preserve the correct charge and pending state.
- No recipe steps were intentionally deferred.

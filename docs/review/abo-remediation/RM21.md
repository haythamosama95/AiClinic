# RM21 — ABO reversal evidence and retry

## Findings implemented

- **R6-BR1:** Provider callbacks now trigger an inquiry and cannot supply reversal amount, parent status, or cumulative evidence. Full reversal requires authenticated original-parent cumulative evidence; incomplete or inconsistent evidence stays `review_partial` and raises an alert. The 400/800 cumulative test confirms the payment remains in review with no platform void.
- **R6-BR3:** Validated provider `refund`/`void` kind is persisted in the reversal row, fact log, and payment history. A same-dedupe-key kind conflict is preserved and alerted.
- **R6-BR2:** Reversal activity no longer suppresses open-payment inquiry work. Six-hour inquiries remain discoverable per qualifying payment and keep the per-payment/bucket cap.
- **R8-RETRY1:** Accepted retry stores an immutable canonical void request before dispatch and batches it with the guarded work transition and local operator action/fact. A parked retry reuses the stored body and version; legacy rows without a request remain parked and return 409.

No recipe steps were intentionally deferred. No D-1…D-6 precedence conflict was found.
